Nimsforest wordmark and lockups
-------------------------------

The wordmark is set in Figtree (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and the composition
of glyph plus wordmark is Nimsforest BV's own. The Apache 2.0 licence covers the
emoji artwork only; it grants no rights in the Nimsforest name or lockup.

Note on exclusivity: no open emoji set is exclusive to us. The distinctive,
registrable part of the identity is the lockup and the wordmark, not the tree.
